# JARVIS — Command Book

*Everything you can say, everywhere you can say it. Pin this. Don't memorise it.*

There are three places to reach Jarvis, and they do different things:

| Where | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Telegram** | running the business from your phone — approvals, switches, status |
| **The app** (desktop/`Ctrl+Shift+Space`) | thinking, showing, doing real work |
| **Voice** | anything, hands-free — same words as the app |

---

## 📱 TELEGRAM — the phone remote

### Decide
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *(tap ✅ on a card)* | approves it — **and if it doesn't send, Jarvis tells you why** |
| *(tap ⏭)* | skips it |
| `/pending` · "pull my drafts" | every gate waiting on you, with its draft and buttons |

### Control the machine
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/autosend on` | approving a gov email now **actually sends it** |
| `/autosend off` | approving only previews — nothing leaves |
| `/autosend` | tells you which it is, and where the setting came from |
| `/kill` | 🛑 **halts every autonomous agent instantly** |
| `/resume` | releases the kill switch |
| `/killstatus` | is anything halted right now |

### Control the team
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/agents` | all 18 — who's on, and how much rope each has |
| `/off Hector` | switch an agent off entirely |
| `/on Hector` | back on |
| `/pause Hector` | stops new work, keeps what you already approved |
| `/tier Hector 2` | 0 = drafts only · 1 = needs your yes · 2 = acts alone |


### Protect your time
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/gate <the request>` | the **true cost** of saying yes — hours, dollars, recovery days, stacking risk — plus a verdict and a message you can send |
| `/cost <the request>` · `/ask <the request>` | same thing |

She also **watches your calendar** every 3 hours. A new event that collides with protected work, could be an
email, or is bigger than it looks gets one ping with a suggestion. Everything else: silence.
*She never moves or cancels anything — she drafts, you tap.*

### Ask
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/brief` | the morning brief |
| `/opps` | the top opportunities, with detail |
| `pursue 1` | drafts a proposal for #1 from that list |
| `/money` | income vs the $10k goal |
| `/capture <thought>` | straight into your vault |
| *anything else* | goes to her brain — ask it like a person |

**What reaches your phone now, and nothing else:** a quote landing · a sub asking something only you can
answer · a decision with money or a deadline · a real error · **one end-of-day report at 8pm.**
Everything else waits for that report.

## 🖥 THE APP — thinking and doing

### Make her show you things
> "put the pipeline on screen" · "put my approvals up" · "show me the team on screen"

A panel materialises. **Drag it by the bar. Resize from the corner. `⛶` fills the screen.** Open several.

Live panels: `pipeline` · `approvals` · `tasks` · `agents` · `money` · `subs` · `record`

### Move what's on screen — voice
> "close that" · "close everything" · "expand" · "move it left" · "next" · "back"

### Move what's on screen — hands
Press **✋** on a panel, then:
- **palm across the camera** → closes it
- **hold still, centred** → expands
- **sweep left / right** → switch panels

*The camera only runs while a panel is open, and stops the moment it closes.*

### Go somewhere
> "pull up my focus tab" · "open control center" · "show me the gov board" · "take me to today"

Instant, no waiting. If a screen doesn't exist she says so rather than guessing.

### Her memory — your vault
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "what do I know about X" | searches 6,000+ notes and **quotes them** |
| "what did we decide about X" | same, from your own words |
| "open my Ana's Care note" | opens it in Obsidian |
| "create a note called X" | writes it, then reads it back to prove it |
| "what did we talk about yesterday" | the unified record — chat, agents, approvals |

### Her eyes
> "what am I holding?" · "read this label" · "look at this"

One frame, on your ask. **Never watching.** Every look is logged with its reason.

## 🧠 The rules she runs on

- **She won't claim what she didn't verify.** If a tool didn't run, she says so.
- **Nothing irreversible without you** — send, submit, publish, spend all stop for a yes.
- **Tier 0 agents are *unable* to act**, not just told not to. It's enforced in code.
- **The kill switch beats everything**, including a Tier-2 agent mid-flight.
- **Money and dates are computed in code**, never guessed by a model.

## 🩺 When something's wrong

| Symptom | Do this |
|---|---|
| Screen is black | wait — it reconnects itself. Or `Ctrl+R`. |
| "I approved it and nothing happened" | `/autosend` — it's probably off |
| She talks over you | fixed; she now ignores her own voice |
| A command did nothing | ask `/killstatus` — everything may be halted |
| Agents aren't sending | `/agents` — check the tier, and that subs are verified |

## ⚙️ Where the real switches live

  (all on / all pause / all off / set a tier across the board).
- **Kill switch** — on that page, and `/kill` from your phone. Same switch, one truth.
