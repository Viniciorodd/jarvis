Camera off. Turn it on when you want Jarvis to look at something.
What this does and doesn't do. One frame is sent only when you press a button — there is no background watching, and the server refuses a rapid loop of looks as well as any "continuous" request. Every look is written to your record with the reason. Gestures are detected entirely in this page from frame-to-frame motion — no library, no model, no network.

Jarvis will not tell you how you feel. Reading emotion off a face is unreliable, and it would produce confident wrong claims about you. She reports what's observable instead — hours at the desk, the hour of the night.
